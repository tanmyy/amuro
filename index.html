120-year cycle

Tap a Mahadasha to see its Antardashas, then tap an Antardasha to see its Pratyantardashas. Dates use a 365.25-day year, the standard Vimshottari convention in most calculators — expect a few days' difference versus software using a 360-day year or exact calendar years. The first Mahadasha is a balance period: only the portion remaining after birth is shown, and its sub-periods are worked out from the dasha's true start before birth, so the Antardasha running at birth is correct.
